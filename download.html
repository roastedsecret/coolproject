Questions

Why is Kova a desktop app?

Signing in and handling contracts happen in the app, where we can protect your account more tightly than in a browser tab. A browser version is planned for later.

Someone sent me an invite link. Do I need an account?

No. Install Kova, then open the link from your email again. Kova opens straight into the deal channel you were invited to. Type your name and you're in. The link only gives access to that one channel and expires after 48 hours. If yours has expired, ask the person who invited you for a new one.

Security

How we protect the conversations and contracts you keep in Kova

At a glance

  • Your workspace's data is stored in Switzerland, in Amazon Web Services' Zürich region.
  • Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Kova staff can't open your contracts without your written permission.
  • Every approval, signature and access link use is logged.

Where your data lives

Messages, files and contracts are stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in its Zürich region, in Switzerland. Kova GmbH is a Swiss company, and Swiss data protection law applies to how we handle your data.

A few things are handled by specialised providers. Payments go through Stripe, so card numbers never reach Kova. Emails such as sign-in links and invitations are sent through AWS. The full list is in our privacy policy.

Encryption

  • In transit: connections between the Kova app and our servers are encrypted with TLS.
  • At rest: stored data, including files and backups, is encrypted.

Why Kova is a desktop app

Signing in and working with contracts happen in the Kova Windows app rather than in a browser tab. That keeps your sessions away from browser extensions and shared browser profiles, and lets us protect sign-in more tightly.

The installer is published by Kova GmbH. You can check its SHA-256 checksum on the download page.

Signing in and access

  • Two-factor sign-in for every account.
  • Single sign-on and automatic user provisioning on the Business plan.
  • Administrators decide who can see each channel and contract, and can remove anyone instantly.

Guest access links

Clients join a deal through a personal access link instead of a password. Each link:

  • opens only the one channel it was made for, never the rest of your workspace;
  • can be withdrawn by whoever sent it, at any time;
  • expires 48 hours after it's sent;
  • is logged every time it's used, with time and IP address.

Records you can rely on

Kova keeps a permanent record of every approval and signature: who did it, when, and on which version of the contract. Business customers can export the full audit log.

Our own access

Only a small number of Kova staff can access the systems that run Kova, and only to keep the service working. We can't open your contracts or read your conversations without your written permission, except where needed to stop abuse or where the law requires it. Access to customer data is logged.

This website

joinkova.eu sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. There's nothing to track you with.

Certifications

Kova doesn't hold security certifications such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001 yet. If your company needs to complete a security review before using Kova, email hello@joinkova.eu and we'll answer your questions directly.

Report a security problem

If you think you've found a vulnerability in Kova, please email security@joinkova.eu with the details. We'll confirm we've received it, keep you updated, and won't take legal action against anyone who reports in good faith and gives us reasonable time to fix the problem before telling others.

Who we are

Kova is made by Kova GmbH, a company registered in Switzerland (company number CHE-382.982.012), with its registered address at Hagenholzstrasse 81/81A, 8050 Zürich, Switzerland. In this policy, "Kova", "we" and "us" mean Kova GmbH, which is responsible for the personal data described here.

This policy explains what personal data we handle when you visit joinkova.eu, download the Kova app, or use Kova as a customer, guest or signer.

We follow the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). When we handle data of people in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we also follow the GDPR and UK GDPR.

This website

joinkova.eu is a simple information and download site.

  • No cookies or tracking. The site does not set cookies, use local storage, run analytics or include advertising or social media trackers.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in server logs for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Downloads. The Windows installer is served from [GitHub Releases / your download host]. That host receives the same technical details when you download the file.
  • Fonts and images are served from joinkova.eu itself, so no other company learns that you visited.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it to reply.

The Kova app

Account information

When you or your company sets up Kova, we collect your name, work email address, company name, job role if you add it, and your sign-in details. If your company uses single sign-on, we receive basic profile details from your identity provider.

Your company's content

Messages, files, contracts, comments, decisions and approvals that you and your colleagues create in Kova belong to your company. We process this content only on your company's instructions, to provide the service. For this content, your company is the "controller" and we are its "processor".

Guests and signers

If someone invites you into a deal channel as a guest, or asks you to sign a contract, you receive a personal access link. Opening it signs you in to that one channel without creating a password. We process the email address the link was sent to, the name you enter, the messages you send, and when and from which IP address the link was used, so we can stop misuse. Access links work only for the channel they were made for, expire after 48 hours, and can be withdrawn by the person who invited you.

When you sign, we record your name, email address, IP address, the time of signing and the document version, so the signature can be verified later.

Billing

Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your billing contact, company address and the last digits of the card or account used, but never the full card number.

How long we keep data

  • Your company's content: for as long as your company keeps it in Kova. After an account is closed, we delete it within 30 days, apart from backups, which are overwritten within 90 days.
  • Signature records: for as long as the signed contract is kept, so signatures can be proven.
  • Billing records: 10 years, as Swiss accounting law requires.
  • Emails to us: up to [2] years after our last exchange.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data, or send it to another service;
  • correct data that's wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to a particular use;
  • withdraw consent you gave earlier.

Email privacy@joinkova.eu and we'll reply within 30 days. Requests are free. If your request is about content in your company's workspace, we may need to pass it to your company, because they decide what happens to it.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), or, if you live in the EU, EEA or UK, to your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA, and we will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

Security

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, limit staff access to what's needed to run the service, and log access to customer data. Kova staff can't open your company's contracts without your written permission. Read more on our security page.
